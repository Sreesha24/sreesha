A lil about me

Hi! I’m Sreesha, a product designer who builds. At 15, I built and coded robots, and that’s when I fell in love with breaking down complex problems. That feeling has followed me through my journey in animation, graphic design and a master’s in Human Computer Interaction.

Over 5+ years, I’ve designed across healthcare, edtech, fintech, entertainment, supply chain and enterprise SaaS. The domains change, but the job is the same: take something complicated and make it feel obvious and fun.

My approach starts with product strategy: finding where user needs, business goals, and technical feasibility overlap. I make data-driven decisions without letting go of creativity and stay hands-on, prototyping and working closely with engineering all the way to launch.

If your team loves pushing what design and tech can do together, let’s talk.
