A lil about me

Hi!! I’m Sreesha, a product designer who loovees to build.

I’ve always wanted to be a designer. At 15, I was building and coding robots, obsessing over how they looked and worked. From graphic design, animation, 3D art, and AR/VR to a Master’s in Human-Computer Interaction, I’ve said yes to projects I swore I knew how to do and figured things out along the way.

Over the past 4+ years, as a product designer, I’ve designed across healthcare, edtech, fintech, entertainment, supply chain, and enterprise SaaS. Every industry has challenged me to think differently, balance user needs with business goals, and get creative within technical constraints.

The more there is to figure out, the more excited I get. And if there’s a new technology to experiment with, chances are I’m already playing with it.

Design became more than something I was good at. It became my favorite way to spend time.
