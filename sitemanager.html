01

Problem

SiteManager had no analytics at all. Enterprise customers managing hundreds of sites had to open them one by one to find leaks, offline controllers and overdue surveys, so critical issues were found late, or not at all.

Our PM had built an AI prototype that proved the demand and set the direction. My job was to turn that idea into a product people could trust every day: decide what matters most, how it’s organised, and how it scales to 300+ sites.

  • Scattered dataSite health lived across separate screens and reports.
  • No priorityEverything looked equally urgent, so nothing was.
  • Doesn’t scaleSite-by-site checks break down past a few dozen sites.

How might we help a portfolio manager know what needs attention in under a minute?

02

Solution

Portfolio overview

One command center that shows what’s wrong across every site, and what to fix first

Issue-first information architecture
Organised around problems, not sites, so nobody has to click through 300 sites to find one leak.
Prioritised by severity
Critical issues rise to the top, so the first thing you see is the next thing to fix.
Readable at a glance
Status uses colour and text labels together, so it works for every user and every screen.

Site drill-down

From the whole portfolio to a single fix, without losing context

Portfolio → site → issue
A clear three-level path, so any problem is a few clicks away.
Context that carries
Filters and selections follow you down, so you always know where you are.
Reports built for action
Survey reports reorganised so field findings are easy to scan and act on.
03

Impact

  • 0→1First analytics experience in SiteManager
  • 300+Enterprise sites visible in one view
  • ShippedBuilt by engineering from my designs

What I learned: an AI prototype is a great way to prove a need, but turning it into a product is about structure. Getting the information architecture right first made every later screen faster to design and build.
